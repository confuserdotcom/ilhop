### The window bounds were measured, not guessed

Recorded here because it is the part most likely to be doubted later:

- **Floor 200ms** — a tight back-to-back same-direction loop costs 12.9–22.3ms
  between repeats, measured over 19 samples. The floor sits >9× above that, so
  a stress test hammering a genuinely correct refusal never trips the override.
- **Ceiling 5000ms** — the smallest gap between two real same-direction
  dispatches in ~4h of organic `ILHOP_DEBUG` logging was 67,514ms. The ceiling
  sits >13× below that.

Accepted and documented cost: a genuinely correct same-direction double-press
inside the window also fires.
