<objective>
Replace the circular landing check, and settle HOP-03.

`il-doctor:110` and `:112` judge whether a hop landed by reading
`$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/il-side` — the file this phase suspects of lying. It can
therefore report a passing round trip while the hop did nothing, which is D-16
exactly and which is why a green `il-doctor` has never been a release gate on its
own. This plan gives the check a truth source that is not the cache, using the
signal `01-A1-RESULT.md` licensed after measurement rather than the one D-14
assumed before it.

Then it settles HOP-03. D-03 corrected the original report: the pointer *does*
land at the centre of the screen; the objection is that screen centre is the wrong
target, which HOP-05 and Phase 3.1 now own. D-18 sets this phase's bar at "lands
on the correct screen and is not pinned against an edge", not a tolerance ring
around a bullseye already agreed to be wrong. The expected outcome is a
confirmation and a recorded finding, and the roadmap explicitly licenses "cannot
reproduce" as this phase's answer. No repair is planned here.

Purpose: every later phase's diagnostics inherit whatever oracle this phase
establishes, and Phase 4's `ilhop doctor` (DIAG-01) builds directly on it.
Output: the landing oracle in `il-cursortrace`, a non-circular `il-doctor --test`,
and `01-HOP-03-FINDING.md`.
</objective>

| `input-leap-server` journal → the landing oracle | Read-only, bounded, after the fact, in a diagnostic tool. The journal stays at `--debug INFO`; nothing here raises the level. |
