<objective>
Finish the refusal log and turn it into a working reproduction surface.

Plan 01-01 proved one exit path end to end. Three silent ones remain — the stale
lock at `il-jump:54`, the superseded sequence at `:168`, and the watcher-unknown
bail at `:169` — and none of them is currently distinguishable from a keybind
that never fired at all. This plan closes that, adds a line on the successful
dispatch path so that the *absence* of any line becomes a usable signal, and
builds the two remaining sanctioned reproduction routes on top: route 1's scripted
stressor, and route 2's passive arm-and-wait.

It also settles, by assertion rather than by argument, the consequence half of
RESEARCH Q2's top-ranked mechanism. `il-side-watch` initialises the side file only
once at process start (`il-side-watch:19`) and reattaches with zero backlog, so a
restart leaves a wrong value untouched and every subsequent press refuses silently
until an unrelated later transition happens along. That chain is provable without
a real hop and without a keypress.

Purpose: D-21 rules out pressing the chord until it fails. Routes 1, 2 and 3 are
what is left, and after this plan all three exist.
Output: complete `ILHOP_DEBUG` instrumentation in `il-jump`, and `il-repro` grown
to the full harness.
</objective>
