<objective>
Say what actually caused it, in a form the author can choose a fix against.

Everything upstream of this plan produced evidence: a measured restart history, a
demonstrated refusal, an asserted staleness vector, a stressed decision table, a
falsified or confirmed oracle, and a settled HOP-03. This plan turns that into one
determination, and it keeps demonstration and inference apart while doing it —
RESEARCH Pitfall 1 is that route 3 is cheap and it does succeed, which makes it
tempting to treat as closing HOP-02 when all it proves is that the mechanism can
produce the symptom, not that it did.

ROADMAP criterion 2 requires the cause traced to a named change or a mechanism
ruled out by diff against the last known-good commit, and criterion 1 explicitly
licenses "cannot reproduce" as this phase's finding rather than a speculative fix.
Both outcomes are acceptable here. A guess is not.

The determination closes by stating what the fix must accomplish as properties
rather than as an implementation. Choosing among implementations is plan 01-06's
first task, where the decision checkpoint sits immediately before the code that
walks through it — D-17 already settled that Phase 1 pays whatever HOP-02 costs,
including if the cause turns out to be `92f64bc`'s toggle and side-file design
rather than a small slip, but one of the candidate shapes changes a contract
Phases 2, 4 and 5 all build on, and that door gets confirmed at the point of use.

Purpose: no code is written in this plan. It exists so that plan 01-06 implements a
determination rather than a hunch.
Output: `01-ROOTCAUSE.md`.
</objective>
